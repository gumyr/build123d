###########
Explanation
###########

Explanation is **understanding-oriented**. These pages step back from the
keyboard to discuss why build123d is the way it is: the ideas it is built on,
the design decisions behind it, how its two modes relate, and what is
happening underneath.

Nothing here is a set of steps to follow, and none of it is needed to
complete a particular task. It is the background that makes the rest of the
documentation make sense, best read away from a design in progress when you
want to deepen your understanding rather than get something done.
