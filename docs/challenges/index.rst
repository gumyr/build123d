.. _challenges:

##########
Challenges
##########

.. image:: /challenges/assets/ttt.png
  :align: center

A way to test what you have learned. Each challenge gives a drawing of a
part. Model the part, and check your result against the mass given: every
drawing states its material and density, and the mass that the finished
part should come to.

These drawings were skillfully crafted and generously provided to build123d by Too Tall Toby,
a renowned figure in the realm of 3D CAD. Too Tall Toby is the host of the World Championship
of 3D CAD Speedmodeling. For additional 3D CAD challenges and content, be sure to
visit `Toby's youtube channel <https://www.Youtube.com/TooTallToby>`_.

Click on a part below to see its drawing. Under each drawing the mass, and
a solution in builder mode and in algebra mode, are folded away until you
want them. The solutions take the density from a material in the
``bd_materials`` package and ask the part for its ``mass()``.
