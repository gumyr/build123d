#########
Reference
#########

Reference material is **information-oriented**. It describes build123d as it
is - the objects, the operations, the classes and their parameters -
accurately and completely, in a consistent layout that follows the structure
of the library itself.

It is meant to be consulted rather than read: you come here while working,
to look up a signature, an enum value or what an operation returns, and then
go back to what you were doing. It states what things do without explaining
why or showing how to combine them; the :ref:`how-to guides <how_to_guides>`
and the :ref:`explanation <explanation>` do that.
